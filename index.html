    Research

    Budget-Coupled Robust Belief Propagation for Visual Hallucination Detection

    When a vision-language model says "there is a dog in this image", how sure should we be? I model the claim and its visual evidence as a tree-structured graphical model, run exact belief propagation, and let an adversary spend a limited budget corrupting the evidence. The result is a probability interval, not a single score.

    A toy illustration of the idea with made-up numbers, not results from the paper. The red half means "below 50%".

    • Inference engine with exact belief propagation, brute-force verification and robust dynamic programming, implemented and tested.
    • Current direction: a robustness audit of when conformal hallucination guarantees survive distribution shift.
    • Next: evaluation on human-labelled claims. No empirical findings yet.
    • With Dr. S. Raja, Department of Mathematics, Amrita School of Computing.
